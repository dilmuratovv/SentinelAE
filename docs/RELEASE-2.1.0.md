SentinelAE 2.1.0: every kind of ESP, not only player ESP. Paper 1.21 through 26.3 (one JAR, Java 21; Java 25 on 26.x).

**What is new**
- **Storage ESP (base finders) is blind.** Chests, barrels, shulker boxes, ender chests, hoppers, droppers, dispensers, furnaces, brewing stands, spawners, crafters, beacons, decorated pots, chiseled bookshelves, jukeboxes and lecterns arrive as air (full blocks as stone, so a wall shows no hole) in the chunk data itself, before any mod can read it, and in later block updates. Each one is sent for real once the player has line of sight to one of its faces within `storage-esp.reveal-distance` (48); one that has been out of sight for 10 s and is more than 16 blocks away is hidden again. Their block entity entries, open/close animations and sounds are hidden as well.
  - The chunk rewrite does not depend on a version number: every known section layout is tried and only one that reads the packet exactly to its end is used (26.x added a fluid count, 1.21.5 dropped the data length). Nothing changes length: palette entries become an over-long VarInt of the stand-in block, direct palettes are rewritten bit by bit. A section update object, which Paper shares between players, is never changed: it is held back for that player and the other blocks are sent one by one.
- **Every entity ESP draws.** New default `protected` groups: players, armor stands, item frames, paintings and other hanging entities, boats and minecarts (also with chests), horses, pigs and other rideables, pets, villagers, projectiles (arrows, tridents, pearls, fireworks, fishing hooks) and dropped items; `mobs` adds every mob.
- **Sound ESP is blind.** Footsteps, hurt/eat/attack sounds, particles and block-break animations of a concealed player are not sent to whoever it is concealed from (Netty filter, no extra plugin needed).
- **Probing is refused.** A hacked client can send clicks at guessed positions within reach to find a chest behind a wall. A click or dig on a hidden storage block that is not really in sight is refused, and nothing is revealed.
- **Never an invisible attacker under load.** Players have their own queue and are checked first with the whole budget; other subjects get what is left (at most `secondary-share`), shown ones are re-checked four times less often, and a crowd arriving at once is checked over several ticks. A concealed player whose check is `fail-open-ticks` late is shown.
- **Ping-aware.** The look-ahead grows with the viewer's ping (up to `max-ping-lookahead-ticks`), projectiles use their exact server velocity, and players riding the same vehicle always see each other.
- `max-distance` 128 (Paper's player tracking range), more work per tick by default.

**Validation**
- 21 unit tests, including the chunk rewriter against every section layout (with and without fluid count and length prefix, palettes of 4 and 8 bits and the direct palette), a buffer it does not recognise left untouched, and the entity groups.
- Protocol bots (`tools/ae_probe.py`, 19 checks; a viewer bot decodes every chunk with prismarine-chunk and logs spawns, sounds, particles and break animations), 19/19 on Paper 26.3 (ViaVersion/ViaBackwards), 1.21.11 and 1.21.1 (ViaVersion): a chest behind a wall arrives as air and a barrel as stone, also in the very first chunks after joining; storage in sight and after the wall is removed arrives for real; a chest placed behind the wall later is not sent; armor stand, boat, item, villager, wolf, arrow and player behind the wall are not sent, one in sight is; a hurt sound, crit particles and the break animation at the concealed player are dropped while the same at a visible spot, and the break animation of the visible player, arrive.
- Real cheat client (`tools/ae_realclient.py`): Minecraft 1.21.11 with Meteor Client, its ESP, StorageESP and Tracers on. In a closed room they draw nothing; through a low glass window they draw the chest, barrel, shulker box, player, armor stand, boat and villager; after walking away and back the storage outlines are gone again. The rewritten chunks load in the genuine client without errors.
- Probing: a click and a dig on a hidden chest behind a wall within reach open nothing and reveal nothing (2 probes refused); the same click in sight opens the chest.
- Together with SentinelAX-ray (Paper anti-xray engine-mode 3 active): protocol probe 19/19 on Paper 1.21.11 and 26.3, and the real LiquidBounce client below.
- Native 26.3 client: LiquidBounce 0.43.0 on Paper 26.3 with SentinelAX-ray active, its StorageESP, ESP and Tracers on: nothing drawn in a closed room, everything drawn through a low window, storage gone again after walking away; no client errors.
- Load (`tools/ae_load.py`, Paper 1.21.11, five viewer bots moving around and behind a wall): 16,384 storage blocks (12,288 chests, 4,096 barrels): average tick ~6.8 ms with SentinelAE against ~5.2 ms without; a farm of 600 items, 120 villagers, 60 horses and 60 wolves: ~12.8 ms against ~9.9 ms, worst tick 36 ms against 39 ms, no player check late (fail-opens 0). The farm test first showed that farm entities could push player checks back (fail-opens 217): players now have their own queue.

**Upgrade:** replace the JAR and restart. An old `config.yml` keeps its `protected-types`; the new groups, storage and sound protection use the bundled defaults until set.
